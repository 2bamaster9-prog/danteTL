DanteJP uses Python, PySide6/Qt, ONNX Runtime, MeikiOCR, OpenCV, NumPy,
CTranslate2, llama.cpp/llama-cpp-python, uv and their dependencies.

Upstream package metadata, source links and supplied license texts are retained
in _internal/*.dist-info. Python's license is in _internal/licenses/python.
MeikiOCR's bundled weights, pinned hashes, sources and license notices are in
_internal/third-party/meiki. llama.cpp and optional compiler-runtime notices are in
_internal/third-party/native; binding notices accompany its package metadata.
PyInstaller supplies the executable bootloader under its distribution exception:
https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html

Translation weights are not included. Optional runtime/model downloads retain
their upstream terms and require an explicit user action.
